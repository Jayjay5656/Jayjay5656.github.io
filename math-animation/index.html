Math-Based Animation in p5.js: Orbiting Dot

A dot orbits a center point using sine and cosine. The angle increases every frame, and the dot's position is calculated with trigonometry.

Controls

  • SPACE or CLICK: Pause / Resume
  • UP / DOWN arrow keys: Bigger / smaller orbit (radius)
  • LEFT / RIGHT arrow keys: Slower / faster orbit (speed)
  • Mouse wheel: Bigger / smaller orbit
